adblock_rules.txt
=================

The bundled DNS block list is derived from the StevenBlack unified hosts file:

  https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts

StevenBlack/hosts is distributed under the MIT License; the individual source
lists merged into it carry their own permissive licenses. The file is used
unmodified except for format-specific lines that this app's parser ignores.

Only DNS-level domain rules are used. No cosmetic (element hiding) rules and no
HTTPS interception are involved.
